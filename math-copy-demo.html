What the eye sees, and what the clipboard gets

Each row renders an expression through the interpreter, then shows the exact clipboard text a reader would get by selecting it (this is the element's textContent — it contains the hidden .copyonly characters and omits the ::before glyphs, so it matches a real copy). That text is then fed back through the parser: round-trips means the pasted formula re-parses to the same thing.
