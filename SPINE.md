# Spine for `intro/`

**Status: the single authoritative plan.** Consolidates the two earlier proposals — the
machinery-earlier spine (rejected) and the cold-open spine (adopted) — and folds in
everything the working-note thread established after both were written. Self-contained:
nothing here defers to another planning file.

Prescription is the cold-open one, unchanged in substance. What is new is §1 (the
diagnosis, previously in a separate file), §6 (every `[RW]` obligation collected in one
place, previously split across two files and referenced by pointer), and §7 (what the
notes changed, which neither earlier proposal could have known).

---

## 1 · The diagnosis

Four defects in the current order. Both proposals agreed on these; they disagreed only
about the fix.

**D1 — The indictment precedes the ontology.**
Ch 2 (`totality-reversibility`) prosecutes absorption using `0·a = 0`, with `0` as the
annihilator (`:62`, `:70`). Ch 5 (`cancellation`) then says `0` was never the annihilator —
`⊘` is, and `0` is one of its two residues (`:56–88`). The founding crime is described for
three chapters in an ontology the book later dismantles.

**D2 — The circle is built on machinery introduced after it.**
`powers-of-zero:50` asserts that grades line up "exactly as the bookkeeping demands" — but
the bookkeeping is Ch 10 (`multiplication`), three chapters downstream.

**D3 — The qualification arrives five chapters after the claim.**
Ch 7 sells `0^ω = −1` as "the choice that closes" (`:74–78`). `where-the-choices-show` then
establishes that `0^ω` is the fingerprint of a *chosen* multiplication (`:116`). A reader
who stops at Ch 9 has been misled by sequencing alone.

**D4 — There is no chapter saying what a legal identity is.**
`x⁰ = 1` and `x¹ = x` are used as forced steps in Chs 7 and 10; `slot-closure` later bars
both as slot-discharges. Nothing tells the reader that writing an identity can be illegal.

## 2 · The prescription

1. **The reader sees the mind-bending thing before any apparatus.**
2. **Machinery is introduced just-in-time, at the smallest size that makes the current
   equation inevitable** — two lines of grade arithmetic where the circle needs them, not
   a chapter of bookkeeping ahead of it.
3. **Honesty is inline, not deferred** — every headline claim carries its one-sentence
   caveat on arrival, with a pointer to the back matter that discharges it.
4. **The formal spine is the *finale*, not the toll booth.**
5. **No monologuing.** Chapters do not narrate their own significance. If a result is
   shocking, show it, then explain it; delete the sentences announcing that it will be.

**How the rules discharge the defects.**

- **D1** — the indictment stops being a standalone act. `⊘` and the fusion diagnosis enter
  *at the moment the reader first asks* "but doesn't `0·a = 0`?" — inside the tour, two
  paragraphs, not a chapter earlier.
- **D2** — the circle chapter introduces grade addition inline, the two lines it actually
  uses. The full bookkeeping chapter comes later and opens with "you have already been
  using this."
- **D3** — `0^ω = −1` carries its caveat in the same breath. No reader can stop reading
  misled. *(Revised: see §7.3 — the caveat's content changes.)*
- **D4** — the rule is stated as a one-line house rule the first time an identity is
  *refused* in front of the reader, and gets its full chapter in the back matter, where it
  reads as the system's deepest property rather than a hazing.

## 7 · What the working notes changed

Neither earlier proposal could have known these. Each is a change to the plan, not just to
the content.

**7.1 — Ch 15's argument is stronger than either proposal assumed.** Both treated the sign
discipline (`=` / `≈` / `:=`) and the bar on evaluation (join-don't-resolve) as two
conveniences to be introduced together. `anchor-shadow` establishes that **evaluation *is*
projection** — discharging a slot out of the language is the act of stepping down a floor —
so they are *one rule stated twice*, by two arguments, five chapters apart in the current
book. Ch 15 should be built on that identity, which makes it a genuine revelation rather
than a collection of house rules, and vindicates rule 4.

**7.2 — Part V/VI splits, and the back matter now ends in a theorem.** `weaves` establishes
that a consistent completion is equivalent to a category of weaves, and that
*information-conservative modelling is precisely equivalence of categories* — which gives
Ch 2's admittedly-informal criterion (`totality-reversibility` fn2, "not yet a fully formal
criterion") its formal rendering. The book can therefore end by cashing the promissory note
it opens with. That is worth a Part boundary.

**7.3 — D3's fix may be the wrong fix.** The plan has Ch 4 introduce `0^ω = −1` with "this
is the fingerprint of a *chosen* multiplication." Two notes complicate that. `anchor-shadow`
argues the value is a **projection**, so the honest form is `0^ω ≈ −1`. And it further
argues that once negation is geometric — a rotational involution, hence the half-turn — the
value is **derived**, not chosen. Those pull in different directions and are not yet
reconciled. **Until they are, Ch 4's caveat should say only that the value is a projection
and that its status is Ch 12's subject** — which is defensible under either resolution, and
does not commit the book to a claim that may be retracted.

**7.4 — Ch 20 depends on a dissolved chapter.** `the-doubling` forces the Cayley–Dickson
step using *Types as Operations*' rule by name — "the type is whatever must exist to keep
the operations working." Dissolving that chapter (§4) is fine, but the rule must survive
somewhere quotable, or Ch 20's central argument loses its citation. Ch 15's opening is the
natural home.

**7.5 — New back-matter material, not yet placed.** Three findings with no chapter:

- The **correspondence principle** — every upstairs distinction must vanish downstairs into
  exactly the classical value. This is the strongest falsifiability guard in the corpus and
  belongs with the badging front-note or in *Judging the Work*, not buried in Ch 21.
- **`ω^ω = 1/(−1)`** — a one-line derivation retiring a question open across six law-sets.
  Belongs in Ch 18 or 19.
- **`i = log₀ ∘ r`**, and that the grade *is* `log₀`. A second, independent route to the
  imaginary unit, built from operations rather than a normalization. This is Ch 5 material
  and would strengthen it considerably.

## 8 · Risks

1. **Double-introduction drift.** Informal grade facts (Ch 6) vs. the full system (Ch 8);
   house rule (Ch 3) vs. full rule (Ch 15). The informal version must never contradict the
   formal one. Mitigation: Ch 15's reconciliation section is a *checklist*, and every
   informal introduction carries a forward pointer so drift is greppable.
2. **The cold open can read as crankery** to a mathematician who opens at Ch 1 and sees
   `0^ω = −1` with no apparatus. Mitigation: the "not a typo" glosses must each state, in
   one line, what kind of claim it is — definition, theorem, or choice — and where it is
   cashed. Use the badges in Ch 1.
3. **Dissolving The Method** removes the one place the epistemics are stated together. The
   badging front-note must survive somewhere unmissable. §7.5's correspondence principle
   would strengthen it.
4. **Part VI is five Wild chapters ending in a retraction list.** That is honest and it is
   also a lot of unsettled material to close a book with. Consider whether Ch 21 needs a
   short closing section that states plainly what survived — the results with computations
   behind them — rather than leaving the reader in the open questions.
