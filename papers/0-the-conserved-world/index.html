The whole theory is the consequence of a single refusal. Insist that no operation ever destroy information — that every operation be total and every operation reversible — and you are not making a wish; you are removing a permission the rest of mathematics quietly grants itself. What follows is what the removal forces, taken one implication at a time, from an axiom you could write on a coin to a frontier where the same structure turns out to be cryptography, and possibly time.

I The one demand, and the one crime

"Total" and "reversible" are not properties of numbers; they are properties of operations. So the honest way to define a world is to stop asking what counts as a number and instead ask which operations it is closed under without loss. That single reframing is the whole starting move, and it immediately indicts a suspect no one usually names.

Everyone knows division by zero is the problem child. But multiplication by zero is guilty of the exact crime reversibility forbids: Solid

a = b ⟶ 0·a = 0·b ⟶ 0 = 0

That step is legal — the same thing done to both sides — and it vaporized everything you knew. Division failing to be total gets a century of fuss; multiplication failing to be reversible gets silence, because we decided that particular destruction was acceptable. Under the demand it is not. And the axiom responsible has a name: absorption, 0·a = 0, the rule that lets zero eat whatever it touches. The entire program is what happens when you delete that one line and refuse to delete anything else.

II Zero is forced open, and the imaginary unit falls out

Remove absorption and the prohibition on 1/0 loses its foundation — because the impossibility of a reciprocal for zero is a theorem, and its proof uses absorption. Take away the premise and the reciprocal falls into the hole it left: Solid

1/0 := ω , 0 · ω = 1 , 0/0 = 1, x/x = 1 with no exception

Nothing was added. A restriction was removed, and the reciprocal was already implied. That subtractive character is worth marking, because it recurs: at every stage the new object is not posited but uncovered once a frozen constraint is lifted.

And ω is not infinity. Solid This is the seam the whole "no limits" claim rests on, so draw it sharply. ∞ is analytic: the endpoint of a process, the limit of unbounded magnitude, the point completion adds to the reals and that one only ever approaches. ω is symbolic: a unit magnitude at an angle — the conjugate direction to 0 — fixed by an algebraic law and reached by no process. Infinity runs off the end of a line; ω wraps around a circle and comes home, 0^(2ω) = 1. The classical reading 1/0 = ∞ conflated the two, because it inverts the shadow — the projected small zero, whose magnitude is already gone — so the missing magnitude blows up. Invert the full edge-on unit instead and you get ω, bounded and clean. So the reciprocal of zero is not infinity; the two look alike only after a projection has thrown the direction away. This is exactly where the symbolic world parts from the analytic one — and the honest home of the limits that genuinely remain, whose job is to rebuild magnitude in the shadow, the one thing ω cannot do for them.

Now push exponentiation to the boundary using only identities that already hold everywhere else — no new axioms, just "apply the rules you believe, uniformly, and don't flinch." Closure forces the values, and the one it forces is the one worth the price of admission: Solid

0^ω = −1 , i = 0^(ω/2) , log₀(−1) = ω

The powers of 0^(ω/2) cycle exactly as the powers of i do. The standard construction of ℂ posits i, defines exp through a convergent series, and defines π through a limit; here all three are derived, and no limit appears anywhere. And the phase is not approximate: the powers of the invertible zero rotate on the unit circle algebraically, their sums satisfying the Chebyshev recurrence exactly, in a ring of exact rational arithmetic with no power series and no branch cuts underneath. The roots of unity are literally 0 raised to evenly-spaced graded exponents. The circle is exact, and it was free.

III The reframe: the shadow and the receipt

Stop and ask why a thing called zero should carry a reciprocal and unfold into i. The answer reorganizes everything before it and everything after. Solid We are trained to read zero as small — the absence of magnitude, the origin where nothing is. Read it instead as a full unit vector seen edge-on: a unit pointing perpendicular to the real line casts a shadow of length zero, not because it is small but because it points in a direction the real line cannot see.

Under that reading nothing is mystical. A unit orthogonal to the reals, with its projection stripped off, is the imaginary unit. The signed zero −0 is a genuinely distinct object with the same shadow, because +0 and −0 are +i and −i seen edge-on. And ω = 1/0 lands where it does because the reciprocal of a unit is a unit and 1/i = −i. The classical zero is this zero with a coordinate projected away — and the thing projected away is not gone. It is a receipt: the information a lossy shadow discards but a conserving world must carry.

The plainest instance is one everyone has used ten thousand times: x^0 = 1. In the conserving reading it is not 1 — it is 1 + 0·ln x, the base's logarithm kept in the grade-+1 tail. And that tail is the derivative: raising to the zero power is differentiation, and the familiar x^0 = 1 is the collapse that burns the derivative and keeps only the ash. True as a shadow, lossy as a fact — the whole program in a single over-familiar equation.

Every classical object is a shadow. The theory is the arithmetic of refusing to throw the receipt away — and the whole rest of this argument is one receipt after another, followed to where it leads.

IV Multiplication is solved; addition is the wound

Carry the grading forward: a value is a coefficient and an integer grade; multiplication multiplies the coefficients and adds the grades; reciprocal flips the grade; 0 sits at grade +1, ω at grade −1, and 0·ω = 1 is just the two grades cancelling. Multiplication is now completely solved, and solved cheaply: its only indeterminate form, 0·ω, is cured by a single integer. Solid

Addition is not, and the reason it isn't is the hinge of the entire second half. Solid Watch a cancellation of leading terms — two graded values whose tops are a and −a. The tops annihilate cleanly (a + (−a) = ⊘, a subtractive cancel in its additive home, no residue), but in a graded world you must know the grade of what is left, and a leading-term cancellation is exactly the event that refuses to say. The top vanished; what remains lives at a higher grade you cannot see without carrying the entire tail. Multiplication's wound closes with one integer; addition's would need a whole series. So the algebra is a multiplicative success and an additive vacancy — and you cannot simply import ordinary +, because ordinary + assumes the ungraded field you just left. The question is forced: can an addition be grown that respects the grading instead of fighting it?

V The wound becomes a dial, and the dial is a chart

Ordinary addition jams because its identity, 0, sits at the very grade that swallows information. Move the identity off that grade — onto the other pole — and the jam clears. Add through the reciprocal: Solid

a (+) b := 1/(1/a + 1/b) = a*b/(a+b)

Now ω is the identity of this operation, 0 is its point at infinity (it absorbs under ⊕, which is a role inside this constructed addition — not the ground-floor annihilator, which is the operation ⊘, never an element), and cancellation lands on ω — a definite pole rather than an unrecoverable grade. The one event that destroyed information became a receipt filed at ω. And this is not a lucky trick but a single rung of a dial: for a real parameter p,

S_{p}(x, y) = (x^p + y^p)^(1/p)

is ordinary addition conjugated by the chart x ↦ xᵖ. Harmonic addition is p = −1; ordinary addition is p = +1; and — machine-checked — multiplication distributes over every S_p, while the sign of p chooses the additive zero, 0 for p>0 and ω for p<0. Solid So "the addition" was never one operation. It is a choice of chart on the output — and that word, chart, is the knob the rest of the theory turns.

VI The chart is the method

Once you see that an addition is a chart, the same chart unfreezes everywhere. Solid The ordinary derivative, the multiplicative (logarithmic) derivative, and the generator of iteration are one primitive read through a chart α:

D_α f = (α∘f)′ α = id → f′, α = log → f′/f, α = xᵖ → the S_p rung

The three "different calculi" are three coordinates on one axis; the compositional one is the additive one in the chart that solves Abel's equation — implicit rather than elementary, but the same slot. This is the point at which the program stops being a pile of results and becomes a method, and the method is one sentence: impossibility is gauge-relative. Plausible Every "must be undefined" or "cannot exist" is a claim that holds only while some structure stays frozen — an identity, a distributor, a coordinate. Unfreeze it and the no-go becomes a coherent transition. The walls are gauge-dependent, and the entire discipline is finding which gauge is frozen and moving it.

Carry the chart up as far as it goes. Solid Over the Cayley–Dickson tower, checked exactly, the harmonic addition stays commutative and associative at every level — through non-commutative ℍ, non-associative 𝕆, the zero-divisor 𝕊 — because it routes through the inversion involution and never touches the product's commutativity or associativity. What breaks is multiplication distributing over it, and it breaks at exactly one level: the sedenions, where the norm stops being multiplicative. So the dial's addition is a fact about the whole tower; its handshake with multiplication is bounded precisely by Hurwitz's theorem. And on the way, the residue: sampling a contour at the roots of unity — the traction phase points — recovers every residue as an exact rational by a finite orthogonality identity, with no limit anywhere, and the 2πi is never computed because it is the winding 2ω, the monodromy of log₀, generated by 0^ω = −1.

VII Obstruct the chart, and a phase won't close

The chart can be written down (the abelian calculi), or left implicit (iteration), or — the case that matters now — it can refuse to exist globally. Where a single chart cannot be made to cover everything, the gauge develops monodromy, and monodromy is one object wearing three faces. Plausible

The residue's 2πi is the monodromy of log around a pole. The octonion associator is a closed, gauge-invariant ℤ/2 sign — checked exactly: unchanged under every basis re-signing, first nonzero at 𝕆, valued in the half-turn 0^ω = −1. The Écalle–Voronin moduli are the obstruction to a global chart at a parabolic fixed point. These are the same object at the unipotent corner — where an eigenvalue degenerates to 1 and the content moves into the monodromy — and the residue's 2πi is its simplest instance. A phase that does not cancel around a loop. The thing the classical theory flees as a pathology is, here, just the chart being honest about the fact that it is only local.

VIII The switch: keep the receipt, or burn it

Now the whole thread ties off, and the figure it ties into is one you could not have been handed at the start because it had to be earned. Plausible There is a single coordinate — the chart α — and everything the derivation passed through is a decision about it:

write it down → reversible calculus, the addition dial leave it implicit → iteration; for modular arithmetic, the discrete logarithm — a one-way function, a trapdoor obstruct it globally → monodromy: the residue, the associator

Keeping the chart makes an operation reversible; hiding it makes it one-way. And for the iteration x ↦ ax inside a modulus, the implicit chart is the discrete logarithm — the coordinate that exists but cannot be efficiently written, which is exactly the trapdoor cryptography is built on. So reversibility and one-wayness are two settings of one switch: keep the receipt, or burn it. The obstruction the analyst flees is the security the cryptographer sells. Traction is the setting that never burns the receipt — which is why it is exact and reversible, and equally why it is not a code-breaker: security is the burning, and this is the algebra that refuses to.

IX Where the thread points, past its proof

Here the rock ends and the rope begins. The derivation keeps pointing after it stops proving, and honesty means marking the difference sharply rather than letting the momentum carry the claims. Wild

Time is the retained order. Commutativity is the lossy quotient that forgets which operation came first; the directed operation is primary, and what the quotient forgets is time — phase accumulated across non-commuting transformations. The block universe is the commutative shadow; non-commutativity does not produce time, commutativity erases it, and the arrow is the global monodromy of the world's non-commuting composition. Bell's inequality is this claim with a number on it: a commuting, order-forgetting hidden variable is bounded, and only an order-remembering one reaches the quantum cos(a−b). Read through the same lens, a photon's polarization — non-abelian where the photon itself is abelian — is the receipt Bell went looking for: not a property the photon carries, but the record of its history, the register where its geometric phase is kept even though its worldline clock reads zero.

The native arithmetic of a reversible world. Microscopic physics is reversible and conserving; traction is the algebra one gets by demanding exactly that. So it is a candidate for the number system physics would have written down starting from the conservation law instead of from counting — the pole a value rather than an infinity to regularize, the phase an exact element rather than a floating-point drift, the obstruction a resource rather than a catastrophe.

One swap in every slot. The 0 ↔ ω reciprocal — acting on the differentials, on the additions as p ↦ −p, on the real forms as Osborn's twist — may be a single involution seen in different rooms. If so, the object the whole program has been circling is not the sum, the differential, or the algebra. It is the swap, and everything named so far is a place it acts.

X The wall the thread is bolted to

A derivation is a chain, and a chain is only as sound as the wall its top link is anchored to. Two claims carry the anchor, and if either fails, the argument above stays internally valid but stops meaning what it claims to mean. Plausible

Consistency — that the collapse-invariants can all be declined together. Absorption, commutativity, associativity, norm-multiplicativity: the theory drops them not one at a time but jointly, and assumes no contradiction follows. This is the ballgame. An inconsistent system feels wonderful until someone derives 1 = 0 inside it, at which point it proves everything and means nothing.

The projection theorem — that the classical world really is this one, shadowed. The symbol 0 is used deliberately rather than renamed to something safer, and that is a promissory note: that classical absorptive arithmetic is this structure modulo the imposition of absorption, with the classical axioms provably recovered. Until that recovery is proven, "the exception for zero is a projection artifact" is a conjecture wearing a symbol rather than a theorem forcing one.

So the honest shape of the whole thing is a single implication with two unproven premises at the top and a machine-checked core in the middle. From "refuse to destroy information," and granting consistency and projection, the reciprocal of zero, the imaginary unit, the exact circle, the addition dial, the tower, the residue, and the monodromy all follow — most of them checked exactly, no faith required — and the thread continues, tagged and honest, into time, physics, and the one-way functions that guard the world's secrets. The theory is not a collection of surprising results. It is one refusal, and its consequences, all the way down.
