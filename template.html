A Section Heading

Body text is set in Newsreader at a comfortable measure. This is the register the whole site speaks in: plain, unhurried, and readable at length. Inline emphasis uses the emphasis class, and inline code looks like reduce(zero). Links such as this one carry the single house accent.

Inline mathematics renders through the expression engine and flows with the prose: the identity a·b sits on the baseline without disturbing the line.

A Subsection

Subsections are lighter than section titles — a shift in weight and size, no rule. The document builds structure through typography and whitespace rather than boxes.

This is an aside — a callout for a remark, a caveat, or a "don't take this too seriously" footnote to the argument. Flat, tinted, marked by a single accent rule.
Displayed Mathematics

Displayed expressions are centered and given room to breathe:

(1) x = –b ± √b2 – 4ac / 2a

The renderer keeps expressions copy-pasteable: hidden copyonly spans reconstruct plain-text operators when selected, so a reader can lift the formula into anything.
